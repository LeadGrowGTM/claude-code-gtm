Speaker Notes

CLAUDE.md files layer. When you open a session inside a client folder, Claude reads that client's CLAUDE.md — but it also inherits from the workspace CLAUDE.md above it, and your global CLAUDE.md above that. Works like CSS inheritance: most specific layer wins, upper layers provide the base. This means you write your identity once at global level and never repeat it. Company context lives once at project level. Client folders only contain client-specific overrides. When Claude needs something it can't find in the current scope, it naturally moves up the ladder. That's not a bug — it's why the system stays coherent across 12 client repos without you duplicating information everywhere.
