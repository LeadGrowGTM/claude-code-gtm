## What You Charge For

**Not:** "AI-powered outbound"
**Yes:** Meetings booked per month. Pipeline generated per quarter.

Clients don't care what's in your stack. They care about the output.
The system is internal infrastructure. Price the output, not the tool.

## The Ramp Argument

Traditional agency ramp: 6-8 weeks (SDR hiring, training, campaign setup)
Operator model ramp: 2 weeks (CLAUDE.md setup, skills adapted, first campaign live)

This is a client acquisition argument, not just a margin argument.
Faster ramp = faster time-to-value = stronger close.

## Pricing Models That Work

| Model | Structure | Best for |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| Retainer | $X/month for Y meetings guaranteed | Established clients |
| Performance | Base + $ per qualified meeting | New clients, trial period |
| Setup + retainer | One-time setup fee + monthly | First engagement |

The setup fee is your margin on the infrastructure build.
The retainer is your margin on delivery.
