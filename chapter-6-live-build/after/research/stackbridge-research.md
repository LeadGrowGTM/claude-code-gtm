## What They Do

StackBridge is a B2B SaaS platform that syncs data between CRMs, marketing automation tools, and data warehouses without requiring engineering. Their core pitch: RevOps teams can set up integrations without filing a ticket. Serves mid-market SaaS companies (50-500 employees).

## Who They Sell To

RevOps Managers and VP of Sales at SaaS companies running HubSpot or Salesforce alongside a data warehouse (Snowflake, BigQuery). Secondary buyer: VP of Marketing when campaign attribution is the pain.
