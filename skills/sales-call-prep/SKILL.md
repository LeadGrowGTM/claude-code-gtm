# Sales Call Prep

Turn sales training + prospect context into a pre-call brief.

## Define

Reads your sales training source (transcript, Hormozi, SPIN, your own notes)
and the prospect's context. Returns a structured call prep sheet.

## Uses

- Pre-call brief for discovery calls
- Encoding a sales methodology into repeatable prep
- Objection anticipation before a specific prospect type

## Insider Knowledge

<!-- Add your own sales playbook notes here after first 10 calls -->
- Lead with their problem, not your solution
- Best opening: "What made you take this call today?" — surfaces real pain fast
- Don't pitch until they've described the problem in their own words
- Common objections for this ICP and how to reframe them:
  - "We're already using Clay" → "Clay is a tool, not a system. What does your system look like?"
  - "Not the right time" → "When would be? And what changes between now and then?"
- Close is always: next step defined, not "I'll send you some info"

## Format

**Prospect:** [name, role, company]
**Context:** [what you know — source, pain signals, tech stack]

**Opening Question:** [first thing to ask after intros]
**Key Discovery Questions:** [3-5 questions to get them talking]
**Pain Hypothesis:** [what you think the real problem is]
**Anticipated Objections + Reframes:**
  - [objection 1] → [reframe]
  - [objection 2] → [reframe]
**Close Target:** [what a good outcome looks like — specific next step]

## How to Use with Training Material

Pass the training source directly:
> "Read [TRAINING SOURCE]. Now prep me for a call with [PROSPECT CONTEXT]."

Training sources that work well: Hormozi's sales content, your own Fireflies call transcripts, SPIN Selling notes.
